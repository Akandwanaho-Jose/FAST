# FAST / MUST Brand Alignment

## Source

The palette and visual roles were verified against the official Mbarara
University of Science and Technology website on 2026-08-01:
https://www.must.ac.ug/

## Institutional palette

| Role | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary institutional colour | MUST royal blue | `#2E3192` |
| High-visibility accent | MUST gold | `#FFD543` |
| Supporting highlight | MUST green | `#8DC73F` |
| Primary text | Charcoal | `#292929` |
| Base | White | `#FFFFFF` |

FAST uses royal blue for navigation, primary actions, major public heroes, and
the administration sidebar. Gold is reserved for institutional bands, active
accents, and announcements. Green highlights content cards, positive status,
and supporting information.

Derived pale and dark colours are used only where required for readable text,
states, borders, and backgrounds. The three official colours remain unchanged.

## Required production artwork

The current `F` badge is a temporary FAST identity mark. Before public release,
replace it with an officially approved transparent MUST logo and, if one
exists, the official FAST faculty lockup. SVG is preferred; a transparent PNG
at least 1000 px wide is acceptable.
